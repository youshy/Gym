# Nutrition & Fuelling — Executable Guide (companion to v6)

**What this is:** the day-to-day *how* behind the fuelling line in the [half-marathon plan](2026-07-29-half-marathon-plan.md). The **Nutrition card in [`v6-calculator.html`](v6-calculator.html)** stays the source of truth for **live grams** (bodyweight + phase → protein / fat / carb tiers). This doc turns those numbers into **when you eat, how you rotate carbs across the week, and what actually goes on the plate** — built around your reflux/hiatal-hernia and your 10:00–18:00 eating window.

**The engine it plugs into (calculator's math):** protein **2.0 g/kg** and fat **0.8 g/kg floor** are *constant every day*. **Carbs are the only lever** — three tiers by day type: **hard 4.75 · moderate 3.0 · easy 2.0 g/kg**. Phase intake: Base/Build **~3400 kcal**, Peak **maintenance ~3700**. **The scale is the arbiter** — losing faster than ~0.4 kg/wk add ~150 kcal, slower than ~0.2 trim ~150; if strength or threshold pace slides, *raise carbs first.* Cut ends at **~88–90 kg**, then hold.

*Worked example at ~97 kg (read your live numbers off the calculator): protein ~195 g · fat ~78 g · carbs hard ~460 g / moderate ~290 g / easy ~195 g.*

---

## 1. Calorie rotation — the three day types

You don't eat the same every day. **Protein and fat hold steady; carbs follow the work.** The point is a **weekly-average deficit that protects your key sessions** — hard days sit near maintenance so you can actually train, easy days carry the deepest cut, and the week nets out to the loss rate.

| Day type | Carbs (calc tier) | Which v6 days | Why |
|----------|-------------------|---------------|-----|
| **Hard** | 4.75 g/kg (~maintenance) | The day's priority session is glycogen-hungry | Fuel + refuel the work you can't afford to run flat |
| **Moderate** | 3.0 g/kg | Ordinary lift + easy-run days | Enough to recover, still a deficit |
| **Easy / low** | 2.0 g/kg | Deload days, pure-easy days | Deepest cut, taken when it costs nothing |

**Which days are "hard" — the disciplined default (3 anchors):**
- **Wed threshold run** · **Sun long run** · **one heavy leg/power day** you nominate (usually the hardest — Thu deadlift or Mon squat).
- Everything else lifting (Tue/Sat press, the non-anchor leg day) → **moderate.**
- Deload week + any genuinely easy day → **easy/low.**

**Phase shifts the count.** In **Base**, lifting is king — you can float a **4th** hard day (e.g. both heavy leg days) if the scale is still moving. In **Build/Peak**, running is king and lifting drops to lean-base/maintenance — pull back to **Wed + Sun + at most one** so the extra running volume, not extra food, is what fills the week.

**Reading it:** tag each day the night before. If the scale stalls two weeks running, **demote a borderline hard day to moderate** before touching protein or fat. If a threshold session or a heavy set feels underfuelled, **promote that day to hard** — the deficit is negotiable, the key session isn't.

**Sample Base week (tags, not grams — grams come off the calculator):**

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Squat | Press | **Threshold** | Deadlift | Cleans+jumps | Press | **Long run** |
| Hard\* | Mod | **Hard** | **Hard** | Mod | Mod | **Hard** |

\* Nominate one of Mon/Thu as your standing heavy anchor; the other floats hard only in Base.

## 2. The daily architecture — your 10:00–18:00 window

**The window is the reflux management.** Food is spread across the day and **done by ~18:00**, so nothing large is digesting when you lie down — the single biggest lever for a hiatal hernia. Spreading also means **no giant protein bolus**: ~195 g lands as **~45–55 g across four gentle occasions** instead of one wall of meat that sits and refluxes.

Four eating occasions around the **11–12 gym**:

| Slot | ~Time | Job | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|------|-------|-----|---------|-------|-----|
| **A — Pre-gym** | ~10:00 | Top off + coffee | ~20–25 g | Easy, fast carbs (day-type flexes this) | **Keep low** |
| **B — Post-gym** | ~13:00 | Refuel the session | ~45–55 g | **Biggest carb landing on hard days** | Low–moderate |
| **C — Afternoon** | ~15:00 | Real balanced meal | ~45–55 g | Day-type dependent | Moderate — some of the fat floor here |
| **D — Final** | by ~18:00 | Close the day | ~45–55 g | Lower (esp. easy days) | Moderate — rest of the fat floor |

**Fat placement:** keep **A and B low-fat** — fat slows gastric emptying (bad pre-training, bad for reflux around effort) and blunts the fast refuel. Park most of your **~78 g fat floor in C and D**, split so neither meal is a heavy single hit.

**Protein placement:** ~20–25 g at A, then ~50 g each at B/C/D lands ~170–195 g. Nudge the three main slots up a little on high-protein days rather than adding a fifth meal — the window stays closed at 18:00.

**Coffee, reflux-smart:** black coffee on a near-empty stomach is the classic reflux poke. **Pair the coffee with slot A's carbs** (they buffer the acid), not before it. If it still bites: smaller cup, or a few bites of food first, or drop to one coffee and take the rest of your caffeine as the pre-run dose on quality days.

## 3. Reflux + hernia — the operating rules

Your triggers are narrow these days — **very fatty / deep-fried food, and really bad-quality chocolate.** So the structure does most of the work; keep these few mechanics and you're covered:

- **Done eating by ~18:00.** Nothing large in the ~3 h before bed. This is the rule that matters most.
- **Stay upright ~2 h after the bigger meals (C/D).** Don't train the evening GtG block on a full stomach, and don't recline right after eating.
- **Portion over bolus.** Four moderate meals beat two large ones — less stomach distension, less reflux, easier protein.
- **Keep A and B low-fat**, and skip the deep-fried / heavy-cream versions of otherwise-fine foods.
- **Bad chocolate out** — if you want chocolate, good-quality dark in a small amount, not the cheap stuff that sets you off.
- Everything else is on the table. This is a *lean* sidebar on purpose — you don't need a long avoid-list anymore, you need the timing.

## 4. Templates + food lists — build your own

Fill each slot from the lists. The template is the shape; you pick the foods.

**Slot templates:**

- **A — Pre-gym (~10:00):** *fast carb + light protein + coffee, low fat/fibre.*
  → e.g. `[fast carb] + [light protein] + coffee`.
- **B — Post-gym (~13:00):** *fast carb + lean protein, low fat.*
  → `[fast carb ×hard-day size] + [lean protein] (+ whey if easier)`.
- **C — Afternoon (~15:00):** *lean protein + carb + veg + some fat.*
  → `[protein] + [carb, day-type sized] + [veg] + [fat]`.
- **D — Final by ~18:00:** *protein + veg + fat, carb lower (esp. easy days).*
  → `[protein] + [veg] + [fat] (+ [carb] on hard days)`.

**Food lists** (all reflux-safe for you; keep fatty/fried prep off them):

| Lean proteins | Carbs (fast → slow) | Fats (park in C/D) | Veg / fruit |
|---------------|--------------------|--------------------|-------------|
| Chicken breast, turkey | White rice, potato | Olive oil | Leafy greens |
| White fish, lean beef | Oats, sourdough/white bread | Avocado | Courgette, carrot |
| Eggs / egg whites | Banana, ripe fruit | Nuts / nut butter (moderate) | Green beans, peas |
| Greek yoghurt, skyr | Honey, jam | Cheese (moderate) | Cucumber, melon |
| Whey / casein | Pasta, couscous | Whole eggs | Berries |
| Tofu, lentils | Sports drink / gels (runs) | Seeds | Squash |

**Flavour without triggers:** herbs, garlic, ginger, low-fat sauces, mustard, small honey, good dark chocolate (small). **Limit / your triggers:** very fatty cuts, anything deep-fried, cheap/bad chocolate.

## 5. How day type flexes the templates

Same four slots — **carbs move, protein and fat barely change.**

- **Hard day:** carbs concentrate **A and B** (fuel the session, refuel fast) and stay present at **C**; **D** carries a normal carb portion. Think two carb servings at B, full plates at C/D.
- **Moderate day:** normal carb at **B and C**, **lighter at D**. One carb serving per main slot.
- **Easy / low day:** carbs mostly **A and B only** (keep training fuelled), then **C and D go protein + veg + fat**, carbs minimal. This is where the week's deficit is banked — and it lands on the day it costs the least.

Rule of thumb: **the harder the day, the more the plate is carbs and the earlier they sit.** Protein slots and the fat floor stay put.

## 6. Around the key runs (reflux-adjusted)

Fold these onto the **Wed threshold** and **Sun long run**:

- **Pre-run (60–90 min before):** **60–80 g carbs + 25–30 g protein, low fat/fibre.** For reflux, lean on **easy carbs that don't sit** — banana, oats, white bread + honey, rice, or a sports drink — over anything fatty or high-fibre. This is slot A, sized up.
- **Caffeine:** 3–6 mg/kg ~45 min pre — **take it with the pre-run carbs**, not as black coffee on empty.
- **During (long runs >75 min, Build onward):** **30–60 g carbs/hour** via drink/gels. Reflux: **sip, don't gulp**, dilute if a gel sits heavy, and rehearse it in training so race day isn't the first try.
- **Post (slot B):** **30–40 g whey + 60–80 g fast carbs**, then a real meal within 2 h. This also refuels the evening GtG block — but eat it **upright**, and leave room before that block.
- **Water only** for anything under 60 min.

## 7. One-glance cheat card

- **Protein 2 g/kg + fat 0.8 g/kg floor = every day.** Carbs are the only dial. Live grams: the calculator.
- **Rotate carbs:** hard (Wed / Sun / your heavy anchor) high · ordinary days moderate · deload/easy low. **Weekly average + the scale decide.**
- **Eat 10:00–18:00, four gentle meals**, ~50 g protein each in B/C/D. **Nothing large after 18:00.**
- **Low fat in A/B**, fat floor lives in C/D. **Coffee with food, not on empty.**
- **Upright 2 h after the big meals.** Done by 18:00 = the hernia rule that matters.
- **Only real triggers:** very fatty, deep-fried, bad chocolate. Rest is fair game.
- **Underfuelled key session or sliding pace → add carbs first.** Scale stalled → demote a hard day to moderate.

## 9. Cheat days, done finely — pizza, burgers, all homemade

A cheat here isn't "blow the plan." It's a **placed treat that fits the math and the reflux** — and **homemade is the whole trick**, because it hands you the two dials takeaway takes away: **how much fat, and how it's cooked.** Pizza and burgers only bite your hernia when they're *greasy and deep-fried*; bake them lean and they're just carbs + protein + moderate fat, which the system already knows how to place.

**The three rules that keep it fine, not a blowout:**

1. **Put it on a hard day.** Wed / Sun / your heavy anchor already budget carbs near maintenance (~460 g) — a homemade pizza's carbs slot *into* that allowance instead of blowing past it. On an easy day the same meal is pure overshoot.
2. **Make it a slot swap, not an add-on.** The treat **replaces slot B or C** — it's that meal, not an extra one. Keep protein high (still the constant ~50 g), let its carbs count toward the day's tier, and just watch the fat so total fat doesn't balloon past your ~78 g floor.
3. **Eat it earlier, stay upright.** Land it at **B (~13:00) or C (~15:00)**, never as the 18:00 close — a richer meal wants the longest possible runway before you lie down. Upright ~2 h after, as always.

**Homemade builds that stay in-bounds (and reflux-safe):**

| Treat | Make it fit | Keep the fat in check |
|-------|-------------|-----------------------|
| **Pizza** | Thin base (your carb) · chicken/lean ham + veg toppings for the protein | Moderate cheese, no extra oil drizzle — *not* deep-pan or greasy meats |
| **Burger** | Lean 5–10% mince · a proper bun (your carb) · **oven** the patty, don't fry | Skip the deep-fried sides — bake potato wedges instead; easy on mayo/melted cheese |
| **Tacos / wraps** | Grilled chicken or lean beef · soft tortillas · beans, salsa, veg | Grill not fry; avoid the sour-cream flood |
| **Pasta night** | Bigger portion + lean bolognese or chicken | Tomato/lean base over creamy/carbonara richness |
| **Fakeaway fried chicken** | **Oven-baked** breaded chicken (panko + spray oil) | The oven *is* the reflux fix — deep-frying is your one hard no |

**Frequency:** **1–2 a week** disappears into the weekly average as long as each is a hard-day slot swap. **The scale is still the arbiter** — if the loss rate stalls, the cheats are the first thing to thin out, before you cut protein or your training fuel.

**What still bites, even homemade:** anything **deep-fried**, genuinely **greasy** portions (the fat, not the food, is the trigger), a **large rich meal late** near 18:00/bed, and **bad-quality chocolate** desserts — good dark chocolate in a small amount is the fine version. Homemade, lean-cooked, placed on a hard day, eaten by mid-afternoon: that's a burger and a pizza that cost you nothing.
