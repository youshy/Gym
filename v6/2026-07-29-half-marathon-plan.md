# Half Marathon — Executable Plan (companion to v6)

**Race:** September 2027. **Targets:** floor sub-2:00 · **primary 1:50** · reach 1:45.

**What this is:** the day-to-day running companion to the v6 program. v6 runs the barbell, the evening pull/push block, and the easy running; this doc adds the **quality running and long-run progression** that turns "runs 7 days/week" into "trains for a half marathon." Open **[`v6-calculator.html`](v6-calculator.html)** for live paces — it re-derives every training pace from your latest 5k time-trial, and your carb-cycled macros from bodyweight. This doc does **not** restate the lifting engine (see [`2026-07-26-workout-plan.md`](2026-07-26-workout-plan.md)) or the design rationale (see the spec in `docs/superpowers/specs/`).

**Weeks are phase-relative** — there are no fixed dates. The checkpoints (monthly 5k TT, the 6-weeks-out rehearsal) keep the season honest against the September 2027 target.

**The one placement rule:** quality runs land **only on Wed and Sun** — v6's no-lift days. Never two quality days back-to-back; never quality within 24 h of heavy legs (Mon squat / Thu deadlift / Fri cleans+jumps). When fatigue climbs, the **evening GtG block is the first thing you cut** (pull-only → off), before touching runs.

## Phase map

| Phase | ~Weeks | Lifting (v6) | Running | Long run |
|-------|--------|--------------|---------|----------|
| **Base** | ~28 | Full v6, incl. the 6–8 wk specialization waves | 1 quality/wk (threshold) + easy base | 13 → 16 km |
| **Build** | ~16 | Lean-base v6: TM frozen, **Fri jumps cut**, evening pull-only/off, no specialization | 2 quality (threshold/intervals + long-run race-pace finish) | 16 → 20 km |
| **Peak** | ~8 | 2 maintenance lift days, no jumps/Oly grind | Running temporarily king; 2 quality | 20–21 km |
| **Taper** | ~2 | Maintenance, lighter | Sharpen, shed fatigue | → ~12 km |

**Two rules that make v6 and this plan coexist:**
- **Specialization waves live in Base.** Run v6's pullup/pec-tri block where lifting is king and running is light. Build/Peak run on lean-base v6.
- **One shared deload.** Every 4th week is v6's deload (5/3/1 + runs −40% + evening →50%). The long-run **cutback weeks land there** — marked ⤵ in every table below.

**Paces:** the calculator is the source. Today (27:00 5k): easy ~6:49 · threshold ~5:46 · interval ~5:29 · predicted HM ~5:53/km (2:04). Season **destination** (off the 1:50 goal): easy 6:15–6:45 · threshold ~5:00 · interval 4:30–4:45 · **race 5:13/km**. Every monthly 5k TT pulls today's numbers toward the destination — always run off the calculator's current values, not these.

## The quality-session library

Every quality run: **10–15 min easy warm-up + 4–6 strides** first, **10 min easy** cool-down. Paces "@ T / @ interval / @ race" all mean *the calculator's current value for that zone.*

### Threshold (T) — comfortably hard, the aerobic workhorse
Progress the total time at T across the season:

| Stage | Session | Total @ T |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Base intro | **3 × 6 min** @ T, 90 s jog | 18 min |
| Base build | **4 × 6 min** → **3 × 8 min**, 90 s jog | 24 min |
| Build | **4 × 8 min** → **3 × 10 min**, 75–90 s jog | 30–32 min |
| Build / Peak | **2 × 15 min** @ T, 3 min jog | 30 min |
| Peak | **20 min continuous** @ T, or **2 × 12–15 min** | 20–30 min |

### Intervals (VO2) — introduce late Base, feature in Build/Peak
- **5 × 3 min** hard @ interval pace, 2–3 min jog
- **6 × 800 m** @ interval pace, 2 min jog
- **8 × 400 m** slightly faster, 90 s jog (sharpener)
- Progress by adding one rep or trimming recovery; **cap hard volume ~5 km.**

### Race-pace & long-run finish — the race-specific work
- **Long run with race-pace finish:** last **3–5 km @ race** (Build) → last **6–8 km @ race** (Peak).
- **Progression long run:** easy → final third @ race pace.
- **3 × 2 km @ race**, 3 min jog (Peak sharpener).
- **6-weeks-out rehearsal:** **8–10 km continuous @ goal race pace** inside a long run — the honest test that sets your real race number.

## Checkpoint calendar

- **5k time-trial every 4 weeks**, on the deload week (fresh legs) → update the 5k input in `v6-calculator.html`; every pace re-derives. This is the single most important habit — it keeps training honest as weight and fitness change.
- **Monthly:** log bodyweight trend and easy-run HR-at-pace (aerobic fitness shows as lower HR at the same easy pace).
- **~6 weeks out (early Aug 2027):** the HM-pace rehearsal (8–10 km @ goal pace). **Race off that number, not today's guess.**
- **Race week:** taper carb-load the final 2–3 days.

## Fuelling quick-reference

Live grams: the **Nutrition card in `v6-calculator.html`** (bodyweight + phase → macros). Summary:

- **Constants daily:** protein ~2 g/kg (~195 g) · fat ~0.8 g/kg floor (~78 g). **Carbs flex** by day type.
- **Carb cycling:** hard days (threshold / long run / heavy legs) ~4.5–5 g/kg (~430–480 g, ≈ maintenance) · moderate (other lift+run) ~3 g/kg · easy/deload ~2 g/kg (deepest deficit). Weekly average lands the loss-rate target; hard days stay protected.
- **Phase intake:** Base/Build ~3400 kcal · Peak **maintenance ~3700** (fuel the build). **The scale is the arbiter** — losing faster than ~0.4 kg/wk add ~150 kcal, slower than ~0.2 trim ~150. If strength or threshold paces slide, **raise calories first.** Cut ends at ~88–90 kg, then hold.
- **Threshold-day timeline:** caffeine 3–6 mg/kg ~45 min pre · pre-run 60–80 g carbs + 25–30 g protein (low fat/fibre) · water only under 60 min · post 30–40 g whey + 60–80 g fast carbs, real meal within 2 h (refuels the evening GtG block).
- **Long runs >75 min** (Build onward): **30–60 g carbs/hour** — practice it in training; it's race-fuelling rehearsal.
- **Two-a-day:** refuel protein+carbs between the midday and evening sessions; ~40 g slow protein pre-bed.

## When it's too much (fatigue knobs, in order)

1. Evening GtG block → **pull-only** (drop push first).
2. Cut evening days **6 → 4**, or off (default in Build/Peak).
3. Drop the Wed quality run to easy for one week.
4. Cut the long run 20 %.
5. Extra cutback/deload week.
6. Drop a lift day early.

The back is the canary: deadlift/cleans yield before the running goal. Never run threshold underfueled.
