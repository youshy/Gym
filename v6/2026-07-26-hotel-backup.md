# v6 Backup — Zero-Equipment (travel / hotel room)

**When:** travel days, no gym, no gear at all. **Assumes literally nothing** — hotel furniture (bed, chair, desk, wall, floor, a solid door) is *fair game but never required*.
**Goal:** hold v6's pillars for a few days — **explosive power, push (chest/tri), legs, pull, core** — and keep the pullup groove alive. Not to progress; just don't lose ground.

**Why this works:** v6's priority is *power*, and plyometrics need nothing — jumps and plyo pushups translate to the road perfectly. Intensity comes from **max-intent explosiveness, reps to near-failure, slow tempo (3s down), and harder variations** — not load.

**The one honest gap:** pulling. With nothing to hang from, there's no true pullup stand-in beyond furniture rows and isometrics. Pull work here is **maintenance** — expect the first real session back to feel off. Normal, and the frequency habit rebuilds it fast.

**Two things run each day on the road, same as home:** the **main day** (A/B/C below) and the short **evening groove block** (its own section at the end).

---

## The 3 Main Days

| Day | v6 equivalent | Focus | Run (if you can get outside) |
|-----|---------------|-------|------------------------------|
| **A** | Tue/Sat (bench + upper plyo + chest/tri) | **Push + upper power + chest/tri size** | 4–5km easy |
| **B** | **Fri (cleans + jumps)** | **Legs + jumps + Oly patterning** — the centerpiece | 3km easy, flat (legs are cooked) |
| **C** | Thu (deadlift + pull) | **Pull + posterior** (maintenance) | 4–5km easy |

Order every day: **warm-up → explosive work first (fresh) → strength → core → run if available.**
Cycle A→B→C→A… across the trip. If a day gets eaten by work, drop it — don't double up.

---

## Day A — Push + upper power + chest/tri

**Warm-up:** arm circles, scap pushups ×10, thoracic rotations/side, 1×15 easy pushups.

| Move | Sets × reps | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Clap / explosive pushups** (plyo — do first) | 4–5 × 3–5 | Max intent, full rest. This is v6's upper plyo. |
| Pushups, feet elevated on bed | 4 × AMRAP-2 | 3s lowering. Elevate feet once flat feels easy. |
| **Handstand-pushup ladder** (chest/tri/shoulders) | 3 × 5–8 | Work your rung: **pike → feet-elevated pike → wall HSPU negatives → wall HSPU → deficit HSPU (books under hands).** |
| **Diamond / close-grip pushups** | 3 × 8–12 | Triceps focus. |
| **Chair dips** (hands behind on a chair or bed edge) | 3 × 10–15 | Triceps + lower chest. |

**Core:** hollow-body hold **or** plank 3 × 30–60s (anti-extension — ab-wheel stand-in).

---

## Day B — Legs + jumps + Oly patterning (the centerpiece)

**Warm-up:** leg swings, bodyweight squats ×15, ankle rocks, deep-squat holds → **jump ramp: pogos → tuck jumps** (a few easy, building height).

**Oly skill patterning first (no load — groove the positions):**
| Move | Sets × reps | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Overhead-squat pattern (arms locked high, deep squat) | 2 × 5 | Snatch-catch mobility + position. |
| Jump-shrug / high-pull pattern (hinge → violent extension → shrug) | 3 × 5 | The clean/snatch pull, unloaded. Explode tall, stay crisp. |

**Jump work (plyo — fresh, max intent, full recovery, land soft):**
| Move | Sets × reps | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Jump squats | 4 × 5 | Reset each rep. Quality over fatigue. |
| Broad jumps | 3 × 3 | Space permitting — measure, try to beat it. |
| Tuck jumps | 3 × 5 | Knees to chest, soft landings. |
| Pogo hops (stiff ankles, minimal knee) | 3 × 20 | Tendon stiffness / running support. |

**Strength:**
| Move | Sets × reps | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Bulgarian split squat (rear foot on bed) | 3 × 10–12 / leg | Brutal, needs no load. |
| Squats, 3s down + 1s pause | 4 × 20 | Or pistol progression if 20 is easy. |
| Single-leg RDL (bodyweight, slow) | 3 × 10 / leg | Hamstring/glute + balance. |
| Calf raises off a step/edge | 3 × 20 | |

**Core:** lying leg raises 3 × 15 (flexion — hanging-leg-raise stand-in).

---

## Day C — Pull + posterior (maintenance)

The hard one with nothing to hang from. Do what the room allows; accept it's holding, not building.

**Warm-up:** cat-cow, glute bridges, big arm swings / pull-apart mimics, prone scap squeezes.

| Move | Sets × reps | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Inverted rows under a solid desk/table** | 4 × AMRAP | *If the room has one.* Best pull available — feet out = harder. |
| **Door-anchored towel rows** (towel over a closed, latched door; grip both ends; lean back and row) | 4 × 12–15 | Vertical-ish pull stand-in when a desk is thin. |
| **Backpack gorilla / bent-over rows** (load it with books/bottles) | 4 × 12 | *If you have a bag to load.* The closest to real rowing on the road. |
| **Sliding floor lat pull-ins** (prone on a slick floor, towels under hands, pull your body forward) | 3 × 10 | Lat activation with truly nothing. |
| Prone Y-T-W / reverse snow-angels (face down) | 3 × 12 | Rear delts + face-pull replacement. |
| Superman holds | 3 × 20–30s | Posterior chain, spinal erectors. |

**Core:** side plank 3 × 30–45s / side (anti-lateral flexion).

## Evening groove block — gear-free (the v6 add-on, on the road)

Same philosophy as home: **antagonist pull + push supersets, always submaximal — if it ever feels like a grind, it has failed its purpose.** ~10–15 min. Keeps the pullup groove and chest/tri frequency alive with zero gear.

**Warm-up (~2 min):** arm swings, scap squeezes, 10 easy pushups.

**4–5 supersetted rounds, ~90s between rounds:**

| | Pull (priority) | Push (chest/tri frequency) |
|---|---|---|
| Work | 4–5 × submax **inverted rows** (desk) *or* **towel door rows** *or* **backpack rows** | 4–5 × 8–12 **pike / HSPU-ladder pushups** + **diamond pushups** |
| Keep | 2–3 reps in reserve, always | clean reps, ~2 in reserve |

**Same 3-load + 1-deload waves as home** (build reps/rounds weeks 1→3, cut to ~50% in the deload week) — travel just swaps the movements, not the structure. Progress the same way: **reps → harder leverage (feet out, deeper range) → tempo.**

---

## Rules

- **Two short sessions/day at most** (main + evening groove). Short on time? Priority moves that cover the most: **jump squats + Bulgarian split squats (Day B), clap + elevated pushups + HSPU ladder (Day A), inverted/desk rows (Day C).**
- **Explosive work always goes first**, while fresh — never grind a jump; if height drops, that set was the last.
- Keep runs genuinely easy or skip them (recovery on the road is worse than home).
- Back on v6's real schedule the day you're home. Treat this as maintenance; the first heavy/Oly session back will feel a touch off. Normal.
